Advertising Privacy Policy

Google AdMob in HAB Labs apps

Last updated: July 4, 2026

Quick summary: Our apps show advertisements through Google AdMob — banner ads and optional rewarded video ads you can watch to earn in-game items. To serve and measure those ads, Google and its advertising partners may process device identifiers and related ad data as described below. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland you are asked for consent first, and you can change or withdraw that consent at any time from inside the app.

1. Scope

This page describes how advertising works in mobile applications published by HAB Labs that integrate Google AdMob, including Vocab Games. It supplements each app's own privacy policy (for Vocab Games, see the Vocab Games Privacy Policy).

2. What ads we show

  • Banner ads — a small ad strip displayed inside the app.
  • Rewarded ads (optional) — video ads you choose to watch in exchange for an in-game reward (for example, free hints). These never play unless you tap to start them.
  • We do not use full-screen interstitial ads.
  • Where an app offers a one-time "remove ads" purchase, buying it turns banner advertising off on your device.

3. Data processed for advertising

To serve, personalize (where you consent), and measure ads, Google AdMob and its partners may automatically collect and process:

  • Advertising identifiers (e.g., the Android Advertising ID)
  • Device and app information (device model, operating system version, app name and version, language)
  • IP address and the general (coarse) location derived from it
  • Ad interaction data (which ads were shown, viewed, or tapped)
  • Diagnostic information about ad delivery
We (HAB Labs) do not receive or store any of this data ourselves. It is collected and processed by Google and, where applicable, its certified advertising partners. We only receive aggregated reports (e.g., total impressions and earnings).

4. Consent in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland (GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the app shows a consent message (Google's User Messaging Platform, IAB TCF) before any advertising data is processed. You can:

  • Consent — allow personalized advertising;
  • Manage options — choose per-purpose and per-partner what you allow (including refusing everything);
  • Change your mind later — reopen the consent form at any time from the app's Settings screen ("Ad privacy options") to review, modify, or withdraw your consent.

If you do not consent to personalized ads, you may see non-personalized or limited ads, which use less data (such as coarse location and current app content) instead of your ad profile.

5. Your choices everywhere else

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID: Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Opt out of ads personalization: in your device's Google settings (Settings → Google → Ads) or at My Ad Center.
  • Remove ads: where offered, buy the one-time "remove ads" in-app purchase.

6. Google's policies and partner list

  • Google Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Advertising — how Google uses cookies and identifiers in advertising: policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • AdMob ad technology providers: the full list of partners that may process data is shown inside the in-app consent message under "Manage options" → partner list.

7. Children

Our apps are designed for a general audience and contain no chat or social features. Ad requests are configured to be appropriate for a general audience. We do not knowingly allow personalized advertising to be directed at children.

8. Changes

We may update this page from time to time. Changes will be posted here with an updated "Last updated" date.
