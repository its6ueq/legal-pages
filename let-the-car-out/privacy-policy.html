Privacy Policy

Let the car out! 3D puzzle

Last updated: August 11, 2026

Quick summary: Let the car out! is an offline sliding-car puzzle game. All 1,000 levels ship inside the app, so it never needs an internet connection and does not ask for the internet permission at all. Your progress and settings stay on your device. This version shows no advertisements, contains no analytics or crash reporting, and has no account system. We run no servers and never collect your name, email, or location.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile application Let the car out! 3D puzzle (package com.hablabs.letthecarout, the "App") handles information. The App is a puzzle in which you slide cars out of a crowded parking lot. By using the App you agree to the practices described here.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Personal Information

We do NOT collect personally identifiable information. The App has no account system, no sign-in, and no chat. It never asks for your name, email address, phone number, or location, and it holds no advertising identifier.

2.2 Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The App saves a single small record in the device's standard local storage. It never leaves your phone and we cannot read it:

  • Campaign progress: the highest level you have unlocked, the level you played last, and the star rating of each level you have cleared
  • Your coin balance, hint balance, and the counters behind the awards - hints used, undos used, clean clears, fast clears, and your best clean-clear streak
  • Which awards you have claimed
  • Settings: interface language, scene theme, music, sound effects, vibration, notifications, resolution, and frame rate
  • Flags recording that you finished the tutorial and whether ads have been removed

Uninstalling the App, or clearing its storage in your Android settings, deletes all of the above. Nothing is backed up to us, because there is nowhere for it to go.

2.3 What the App Does NOT Do

  • No internet access. The App does not declare the INTERNET permission. Levels, translations, sounds, and artwork are all bundled or generated on the device.
  • No advertisements. This version serves no banner, interstitial, or rewarded ad, and includes no advertising SDK.
  • No live in-app purchases. The shop in this version is a local coin economy and charges you nothing.
  • No analytics, telemetry, or crash reporting. We collect no usage statistics of any kind.
  • No access to photos, contacts, camera, microphone, files, or location.

2.4 Permissions the App Requests

  • Vibrate (android.permission.VIBRATE) - short taps of haptic feedback when a car snaps into place, when a move is blocked, and when you clear a level. Turn it off in Settings and the App stops vibrating.

3. How Information Is Used

The locally stored record exists for one purpose: so the game can remember where you were and how you like to play. It is not transmitted, aggregated, profiled, or sold.

4. Third-Party Services

This version of the App integrates no third-party advertising, analytics, or backend service. The only third party involved is the store you installed from:

  • Google Play handles the download, update, and refund of the App under its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

The App is built with the Unity engine. Unity's runtime is compiled into the App; no Unity analytics, ads, or services package is included in this build.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your data, because we never receive any. No data leaves your device.

6. Your Choices

  • Delete everything: clear the App's storage, or uninstall it, to remove all progress and settings.
  • Reset progress in-game: Settings contains a reset that clears your save.
  • Turn off vibration, music, or sound: Settings, at any time.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is family-friendly. It has no chat, no user-generated content, no social features, and no advertising, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us.

8. Data Security

  • Progress and settings are kept in the device's standard app-private local storage, which other apps cannot read.
  • We operate no servers, so there is no account of yours for us to lose and no database of ours to breach.

9. Future Versions

A later version may add optional features that do involve a third party - for example Google Play Games sign-in for achievements and a leaderboard, in-app purchases through Google Play billing, or advertising. If that happens, this page will be updated to describe it before that version ships, and the "Last updated" date above will change.

10. Changes to This Policy

Changes are posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date. Continuing to use the App after a change means you accept the revised policy.
