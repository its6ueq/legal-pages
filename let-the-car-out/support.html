Support

Let the car out! 3D puzzle

Last updated: 17 August 2026

Need a hand? Write to us and we will answer. There is one person behind this app, so a reply usually takes a day or two rather than an hour.

1. My progress disappeared

Progress is kept on the device. If you reinstalled the app, changed phone, or cleared its storage, the local copy is gone.

It can be recovered only if you were signed in to Google Play Games (Android) or Game Center and iCloud (iOS) before, and the cloud save had run at least once. Sign in with the same account and open the app; it pulls the saved copy and merges it with whatever is on the device, keeping the better of the two for every level.

2. Sign-in does not complete

On Android this is almost always the Google Play Games app rather than the game: open it, check you are signed in there, and make sure the account is not one restricted by a family group.

If the game was installed from a source other than Google Play, sign-in cannot work at all - Play Games checks the signature of the installed app.

3. The phone does not vibrate

Check Settings inside the game, then your phone's own vibration setting. Some phones also disable app vibration in battery-saver mode.

Tablets and some older phones have no vibration motor and cannot vibrate at all.

4. A purchase did not arrive

Purchases are delivered by the store. Reopen the app while connected: the store re-sends anything it has taken payment for but not yet delivered, and the app grants it on the spot.

If it still has not arrived after a few minutes, send us the order number from your Google Play or App Store receipt and we will sort it out. Refunds have to go through the store you bought from - we cannot issue them.

5. A level looks impossible

Every level in the game has been solved by machine before shipping, so there is always a way out. The hint button shows the next move on the shortest known solution. If you believe a level really cannot be finished, tell us its number and we will check that one.

6. Deleting your data

Clear the app's storage or uninstall it to remove the local save. To remove the cloud copy, delete the saved game in the Google Play Games app, or the app's iCloud data in iOS Settings.
