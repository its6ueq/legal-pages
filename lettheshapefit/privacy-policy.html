Privacy Policy

Let The Shape Fit! · HAB Labs · Updated October 19, 2026

You can play without creating an account. The app includes Google AdMob advertising, an optional Remove Ads purchase, and Google Play Games on Android or Game Center and iCloud on iOS. Availability of these services depends on the platform and release.

Data stored on your device

The app stores the board you are playing, the furthest level completed, game statistics (wins, drags, undos, hints used, daily streak and last play day), unlocked badges, your hint balance, tutorial state, and preferences such as theme, colors, sound and vibration. The 5,000 levels ship inside the app. HAB Labs does not operate a server that receives your progress.

Advertising and consent

Google AdMob provides banner ads and videos you choose to watch for hints. The app requests non-personalized ads. This does not eliminate data processing: Google's advertising SDK may collect and share IP addresses (including approximate location inferred from them), advertising or app/device identifiers, ad impressions and clicks, app interaction information, and diagnostic or performance information. Google uses these data for ad delivery, measurement, analytics, security and fraud prevention under its Privacy Policy and Mobile Ads data disclosure.

The app uses Google's User Messaging Platform to check whether ads may be requested and to show privacy messages where required. When available, Settings offers ad privacy choices. You can also manage advertising identifiers in your device's privacy settings. Buying Remove Ads turns off banner ads; reward videos stay available only when you choose to watch one.

Purchases

Remove Ads is a one-time purchase processed by Google Play or Apple's App Store. The app receives the product identifier, transaction status and purchase token needed to deliver or restore it. HAB Labs never receives your card number or banking details. The store keeps its own purchase records under its privacy policy. Restore Purchases restores Remove Ads on the same store account.

Game services and saved progress

When you sign in, Google Play Games on Android, or Game Center on iOS, links submitted achievements and leaderboard scores (levels solved and best streak) to your platform game profile. The platform may show your profile and scores to other players according to its settings. We do not send your game-service identity or saved progress to AdMob.

Where cloud saving is available, the app sends your completed level, game statistics, unlocked badges, hint balance and tutorial state to Google Play Games Saved Games (stored in Google Drive) or Apple iCloud, so progress can be restored on another device using the same account. The board in play is not included. Google and Apple process this data under their own policies: Google and Apple.

Support and external links

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send, and use them only to answer you. Please do not send passwords or payment details. Opening an external link uses your browser and the destination's privacy practices. The app does not use analytics, crash reporting, accounts or notifications.

Your choices, retention and deletion

Local data stays until you reset progress in Settings or clear the app's data. Uninstalling or clearing data does not delete records held by Google or Apple, such as purchase history, achievements or saved games; manage those in Google Play Games, Google Drive or your Apple account settings. For questions about information you sent us, contact shawnfrest2k5@gmail.com.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Security and changes

The integrated services use encrypted connections. No service can be guaranteed completely secure. We will update this page when the app's data practices change. Developer: HAB Labs. Privacy contact: shawnfrest2k5@gmail.com.
