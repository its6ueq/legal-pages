Privacy Policy

All-in-one apps

Last updated: June 24, 2026

Quick summary: All-in-one apps is an offline utility toolbox. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your data stays on your device.

1. Introduction

All-in-one apps ("we", "our", or "the App") is a collection of 47 utility tools (generators, converters, sensors, calculators and text tools). This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles your information. We built it to respect your privacy completely.

2. Information We Collect

None. The App does not create an account, does not ask for personal information, and contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. We have no servers that store your data.

  • No name, email, phone number or location is collected.
  • No advertising identifiers are used.
  • No usage analytics or crash-reporting services are integrated.

3. Local Data

Some tools save data only on your device using local storage:

  • Quick Notes you create.
  • Pinned tools and your theme / language settings.

This data never leaves your device and is removed when you uninstall the App.

4. Internet Usage

Almost every tool runs fully offline. Only two optional tools make a network request, and only when you open them:

  • Currency converter — fetches public exchange rates from a free, open API.
  • Speed Test — downloads a test file to measure your connection.

These requests contain no personal data. If you never open these two tools, the App makes no network connections.

5. Device Sensors

Tools such as the Bubble Level, Compass, Protractor and Vibration meter read your device's motion and orientation sensors in real time. This sensor data is processed on-device only and is never stored or transmitted.

6. Permissions

The App requests a single permission: Internet, used solely for the two optional online tools listed in Section 4.

7. Children's Privacy

The App collects no data and shows no ads, making it safe for users of all ages.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy and will revise the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.
