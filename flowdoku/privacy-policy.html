Privacy Policy

FlowDoku - Flower Puzzle

Last updated: September 28, 2026

Quick summary: FlowDoku is an offline logic puzzle. The current app stores your board, progress, settings, and sunshine balance on your device. Advertising, in-app purchases, account sign-in, Play Games, Game Center, and cloud sync are disabled in the current build. We do not operate a gameplay server or collect your name, email address, or location through the app.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile application FlowDoku - Flower Puzzle (the "App") handles information. FlowDoku is a garden logic puzzle: place exactly one flower in each color region, at most one flower in each row and column, and keep flowers from touching, even diagonally. By using the App you agree to the practices described here.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Personal Information

We do not collect personally identifiable information through the current app. FlowDoku does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or location, and account sign-in is disabled. If you email support, we receive the information you choose to send in that email.

2.2 Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The App saves the following on your device only, using the device's standard local storage. We cannot read it:

  • Settings: theme (light / dark), accent color, sound, haptics, and interface language
  • Which levels you have completed, your stars, and your best times
  • Your sunshine balance, daily gift date, and the levels where blocked-cell display was unlocked
  • Your active puzzle, marks, flowers, mistakes, hints, and remaining lives
  • Whether you have completed the interactive tutorial, and your place in it

Uninstalling the App, or clearing its storage in your Android or iOS settings, deletes all of the above.

2.3 Advertising

Advertising is disabled in the current build. FlowDoku does not request or show AdMob ads during normal play. If advertising is enabled in a future version, we will update this policy and the store disclosures before that version is released.

2.4 In-App Purchases

In-app purchases are disabled in the current build. If sun packs or a Remove Ads product become available in a future version, billing will be handled by Google Play or Apple. We will update this policy before enabling purchases.

2.5 Play Games and Game Center

Play Games, Game Center, and cloud sync are disabled in the current build. The achievements and scores shown in the app use progress stored locally on your device. They do not restore your progress after reinstalling or transfer it to another device.

2.6 What the App Does NOT Do

  • No analytics or crash-reporting service. We collect no usage statistics ourselves.
  • No access to photos, contacts, camera, microphone, or files.
  • No social features, no chat, no user-generated content.
  • No network access is needed to play. Puzzle content is available offline. Opening a legal or support link uses your browser and an internet connection.

3. How Information Is Used

The current app uses local data to resume your puzzle, show progress and achievements, remember your settings, and manage sunshine spent on hints and other power-ups.

4. Data Sharing

The current app does not transmit your gameplay progress or sunshine balance to us or a game service. If you open a legal or support link, your browser contacts our pages hosted by GitHub Pages under its own privacy practices. If you email support, your email provider sends the message and the information you include to us.

5. Your Choices

  • Play offline: no account or internet connection is required for gameplay.
  • Delete local data: clear the app's storage or uninstall it to remove local settings, progress, and sunshine. This also removes progress that has not been backed up elsewhere.
  • Contact us: email us if you have a question about data or the app.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is family-friendly. It contains no chat, no user-generated content, and no social features, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us.

7. Data Security

  • Settings, active puzzle state, progress, and sunshine are kept in the device's standard local storage.
  • We operate no gameplay server and the current app has no account or cloud save.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the App changes. Changes are posted here with a new "Last updated" date, and the stores' listing pages always link to the current version.
