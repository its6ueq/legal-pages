Common questions

How do I play?

Place exactly one flower in each colored region, with at most one flower in each row and column. Flowers cannot touch, even diagonally. Tap once to mark a cell with X, swipe horizontally to mark a row, swipe vertically to mark a column, and double-tap to plant or remove a flower. The in-game How to play guide walks through the rules on a real board.

Does the game need an internet connection?

No. Puzzle content and progress work offline. Opening the legal or support pages uses your browser and an internet connection.

Do I need an account?

No. The game never asks you to register, and it works fully without signing in to anything.

What are the suns?

Sunshine is an in-game resource for hints, revealing blocked cells, and revives. You start with three and may claim one daily gift that does not stack. Ads and real-money sun packs are disabled in the current build.

What does a hint do?

A hint (the lightbulb) highlights a cell where a flower can definitely go right now. The dots power-up (the ··· button) marks all cells a placed flower blocks for the rest of the level - it costs one sun per level and stays on for that level.

I lost my progress after reinstalling

Progress is stored on your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes it. Play Games, Game Center, and cloud backup are disabled in the current build, so the app cannot restore lost local progress after a reinstall.

Can I buy sunshine or remove ads?

No. Advertising and in-app purchases are disabled in the current build. If you have an order receipt from an earlier test version, email us with the order number so we can investigate.

How do I delete my data?

Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes local game data. The current build does not create a cloud save. Details are in the privacy policy.
