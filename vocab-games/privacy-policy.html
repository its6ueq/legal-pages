Privacy Policy

Vocab Games

Last updated: June 19, 2026

Quick summary: Vocab Games is an offline word-puzzle app. Your settings and game progress are stored only on your device. We do not require sign-in and we do not run any servers. The app shows advertisements through Google AdMob, which uses device identifiers to serve ads.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile application Vocab Games (the "App"), which contains the games VocalGrid and VocabLine, handles information. By using the App you agree to the practices described here.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Personal Information

We do NOT collect any personally identifiable information. The App has no account or login and does not ask for your name, email, phone number, or location.

2.2 Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The App saves the following on your device only (using local storage). We cannot access it:

  • Game settings (theme, accent color, language, word length, sound/haptics, dark mode)
  • Game progress (current streak, best score, VocabLine levels passed)
  • Whether the first-time tutorial has been shown

2.3 Information Collected by Advertising

The App displays ads using Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, AdMob and Google may automatically collect:

  • Advertising identifiers (e.g., Android Advertising ID)
  • Approximate device and app information (device model, OS version, app version)
  • IP address and general (coarse) location derived from it
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)

3. How Information Is Used

  • To run the games and remember your settings and progress (stored locally)
  • To display advertisements and measure their performance
  • To keep the app stable and improve it

4. Third-Party Services

4.1 Google AdMob

We use Google AdMob to show advertisements. AdMob may collect and process data as described in Google's policies:

  • Data collected: device identifiers, IP address, ad interaction and app usage data
  • Purpose: to serve personalized or non-personalized advertisements and measure them
  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. The only data leaving your device is the advertising data described above, which is processed by Google AdMob to serve ads. We do not operate our own servers and do not transmit your settings or progress anywhere.

6. Your Choices

  • Reset advertising ID: Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Opt out of personalized ads: in your device's Google ad settings; you may then see non-personalized ads.
  • Delete local data: clear the app's storage or uninstall the App to remove all locally stored settings and progress.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is family-friendly and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. It contains no chat or social features. Because the App shows ads, we configure ad requests to be appropriate for a general audience. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us.

8. Data Security

  • Settings and progress are stored using the device's standard local storage.
  • No personal data is transmitted to or stored on our servers (we have none).

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

11. Compliance

This Privacy Policy is intended to comply with Google Play policies, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).
