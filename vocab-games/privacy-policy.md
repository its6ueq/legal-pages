# Privacy Policy — Vocab Games

_Last updated: June 19, 2026_

**Quick summary:** Vocab Games is an offline word-puzzle app (containing the games VocalGrid and VocabLine). Your settings and game progress are stored only on your device. There is no sign-in and we run no servers. The app shows advertisements through Google AdMob, which uses device identifiers to serve ads.

## 1. Information We Collect

**Personal information:** None. The app has no account/login and does not ask for your name, email, phone number, or location.

**Stored locally on your device only** (we cannot access it):
- Settings (theme, accent color, language, word length, sound/haptics, dark mode)
- Game progress (streak, best score, VocabLine levels passed)
- Whether the tutorial has been shown

**Collected by advertising (Google AdMob):**
- Advertising identifiers (e.g., Android Advertising ID)
- Device and app info (model, OS version, app version)
- IP address and coarse location derived from it
- Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)

## 2. How Information Is Used
- To run the games and remember your settings/progress (locally)
- To display and measure advertisements
- To keep the app stable and improve it

## 3. Third-Party Services
The app uses **Google AdMob** to show ads. AdMob may collect device identifiers, IP address, and usage/ad-interaction data to serve personalized or non-personalized ads.
- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- How Google uses data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

## 4. Data Sharing
We do not sell your data. The only data leaving your device is the advertising data above, processed by Google AdMob. We operate no servers and do not transmit your settings or progress anywhere.

## 5. Your Choices
- Reset advertising ID: Android Settings → Privacy → Ads
- Opt out of personalized ads in your device's Google ad settings
- Delete local data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app

## 6. Children's Privacy
The app is family-friendly, has no chat/social features, and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ad requests are configured to be appropriate for a general audience.

## 7. Data Security
Settings and progress use the device's standard local storage. No personal data is sent to or stored on our servers (we have none).

## 8. Changes
We may update this policy; changes are posted here with a new "Last updated" date.
