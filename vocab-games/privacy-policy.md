# Privacy Policy — Vocab Games

_Last updated: June 26, 2026_

**Quick summary:** Vocab Games is a word-puzzle app with several mini-games. Your settings and game progress are stored on your device. The app shows advertisements through Google AdMob, offers optional in-app purchases, and can optionally connect to Google Play Games to sync achievements, leaderboards, and cloud-saved progress if you choose to sign in. We run no servers of our own and do not collect your name, email, or precise location.

The App includes the games VocabGrid, VocabLine, VocabBee, VocabStep, VocabHunt, VocabChain, VocabJumble, VocabClue, VocabTwin, and VocabFlip.

## 1. Information We Collect

**Personal information:** None collected by us. The app has no account of its own and does not ask for your name, email, phone number, or precise location. If you choose to sign in to Google Play Games, Google associates your gameplay with your Google account; we do not receive your identity.

**Stored locally on your device only** (we cannot access it):
- Settings (theme, accent color, interface language, game language, difficulty mode, word length, sound/haptics, dark mode)
- Game progress (streaks, best scores, levels passed, ladders solved, boards cleared, daily puzzle state)
- Saved words you bookmark from the in-app dictionary
- Your lightbulb (hint) balance and whether you bought "remove ads"
- A background image you optionally choose from your device
- Whether the tutorial has been shown

**Collected by advertising (Google AdMob)** — including optional rewarded ads you may watch to earn hints:
- Advertising identifiers (e.g., Android Advertising ID)
- Device and app info (model, OS version, app version)
- IP address and coarse location derived from it
- Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)

**In-app purchases (Google Play Billing):** A one-time "remove ads" purchase and consumable hint packs are processed by Google Play. Google handles payment; we receive only a purchase confirmation/token to unlock the item, never your card or payment details.

**Google Play Games Services (optional sign-in):** If you sign in, Google stores your achievements, leaderboard scores, and cloud-saved game progress under your Google account so it can sync across devices. You can skip sign-in and play fully offline.

**Photos / media:** If you set a custom background, the app reads the image you pick. It stays on your device and is never uploaded.

## 2. How Information Is Used
- To run the games and remember your settings/progress (locally)
- To display and measure advertisements
- To process in-app purchases and unlock what you bought
- To sync achievements, leaderboards, and saved games when you sign in to Google Play Games
- To keep the app stable and improve it

## 3. Third-Party Services
- **Google AdMob** — shows ads; may collect device identifiers, IP address, and usage/ad-interaction data to serve personalized or non-personalized ads.
- **Google Play Billing** — processes in-app purchases.
- **Google Play Games Services** — stores achievements, leaderboard scores, and cloud saves under your Google account when you sign in.

Links: Google Privacy Policy — https://policies.google.com/privacy · How Google uses data — https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

## 4. Data Sharing
We do not sell your data. Data leaving your device is limited to: advertising data processed by Google AdMob; purchase confirmations processed by Google Play Billing; and, only if you sign in, the achievements, leaderboard scores, and cloud-saved progress handled by Google Play Games Services. We operate no servers of our own and do not transmit your settings, saved words, or chosen background image anywhere.

## 5. Your Choices
- Reset advertising ID: Android Settings → Privacy → Ads
- Opt out of personalized ads in your device's Google ad settings
- Remove ads via the one-time in-app purchase
- Play Games: skip sign-in (the app works fully offline), or manage/delete your saved games and scores in the Google Play Games app
- Delete local data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app

## 6. Children's Privacy
The app is family-friendly, has no chat/social features, and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ad requests are configured to be appropriate for a general audience.

## 7. Data Security
Settings, progress, and saved words use the device's standard local storage. No personal data is sent to or stored on our servers (we have none). Cloud saves, when enabled, are stored by Google under your account.
